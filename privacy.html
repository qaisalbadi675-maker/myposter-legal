Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

MyPoster is a personal-use application. This policy explains what data it accesses and how it is handled.

1. What data is accessed

When you connect a platform (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram), MyPoster requests only the minimum permissions needed to upload video content on your behalf, via each platform's official OAuth login system.

2. What data is stored

Login tokens are stored locally in the app's session to allow uploads without repeated logins. Video files are temporarily held during the upload process and deleted immediately afterward. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

3. Third parties

MyPoster interacts only with the official APIs of YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram to perform uploads you initiate. No other third parties receive your data.

4. Data deletion

You can disconnect any platform at any time, which removes stored access tokens for that platform.

Contact

For questions, contact the app owner directly.
